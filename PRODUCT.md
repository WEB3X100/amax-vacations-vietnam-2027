# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Canadian leisure travellers, initially browsing from Toronto, who want an all-inclusive package and prefer to speak with a travel advisor before booking. Their job is to compare destination options, understand the shape of a package, and submit enough information for an AMAX representative to follow up by phone or WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

AMAX Vacations uses a visually credible static catalogue to build trust, present package options by country, and convert interest into qualified enquiries. Success is an enquiry with a traveller's name, phone, email, and destination interest—not an online checkout.

## Positioning

AMAX combines supplier package inventory with human advisor-led follow-up. The website helps a visitor narrow the trip; an AMAX representative confirms current availability, inclusions, and price.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from multi-package campaign ads and browse country collections.
- Country boxes lead into package details and a shared enquiry flow.
- WhatsApp is the fastest initial conversion path.
- A sales representative follows up by phone to qualify and complete the booking.
- Live package search through Softvoyage is deferred until traffic justifies the cost.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no framework or checkout engine.
- Country filters, package-detail views, WhatsApp conversion, and a lead enquiry form.
- Current prices and supplier availability are not connected; the draft must not fabricate live inventory or guaranteed prices.
- Supplier-provided Sunwing and Air Transat materials may inform future approved package content, but third-party creative is not bundled into this draft.
- The existing Vietnam 2027 scroll experience remains preserved on the repository's `main` branch.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: AMAX Vacations.
- Use the supplied AMAX logo and the established deep-teal, warm-cream, tropical-magenta identity.
- Feel vivid, youthful, premium, and trustworthy rather than like a generic discount aggregator.
- Structural references: Sunwing package discovery, Transat premium collections, Flight Centre detail hierarchy, and Marlin Travel destination-led browsing.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing AMAX logo and Vietnam 2027 imagery in `assets/`.
- User-supplied AMAX visual references in the active design brief.
- Original AMAX campaign imagery generated for this branch in `assets/campaign/`.
- Meeting summary confirming the country-package-detail-form flow, WhatsApp conversion, sales follow-up, and deferred live search.
- No approved supplier package feed, live prices, testimonials, ratings, or booking availability is present in this repository.

## Product Principles

1. Show the escape quickly, then make the next action obvious.
2. Organize discovery around countries and a few understandable packages.
3. Be explicit when rates or availability require advisor confirmation.
4. Make human follow-up feel like a benefit, not a missing checkout feature.
5. Preserve a fast, accessible experience across phone and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic form labels and errors, reduced motion, readable contrast, and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
